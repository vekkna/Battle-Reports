The War Garage: Battle Report 1

Background

Although the High Elves sailed for Ulthuan millennia ago, they still maintain outposts in the Old World to protect the geomantic waystones that help slow the spread of Chaos.

Skaven Clan Leader Horgan eyed one such outpost as a valuable prize. Uunder cover of darkess, he launched a vicious surprise attack from the neighbouring forest. However, little did he realise that through necromantic interrogation, the ancient liche Sinistrum had learned of the his plans. Sinistrum and his close ally Warboss Jed were lying in ambush, waiting for the two sides to exhaust themselves before taking the prize as their own.

Spotting the danger just in time, High Elf Lord Ryder and Horgan realised that their survival depended on their fighting side by side, at least for now.

The Skaven artillerists on the right flank edge their cautious way towards a nearby hill, from which they gleefully plan to rain burning warpfire upon the flesh of their hated foes.

They are supported by the thundering Doom Wheel and ferocious Rat Ogres.

Undaunted at the prospect of a burning doom, the orcish spears and boar riders (too unimaginative) and the undead chariot (no flesh to burn) advance along the nearby town’s outskirts. The chariot pauses long enough to secure a waystone.

The skies darken beneath the leathery black wings of the vampire bats, who shelter behind a farmer’s cottage.

The proud High Elf spearmen march forward under the banner of their lord.

Behind them, their unlikely allies the Skaven Jezzails, let fly with their long-range rifles. Results are mixed. The newest warpstone/gunpowder mixture is not as stable as their engineers assured them, and while an orc standard bearer is downed, the guns of two Skaven shooters blow up in their faces.

Sneering at the grisly fate of the Jezzails, the shadowy Night Scouts use the distraction of the explosions to sneak forwards and secure the tower.

Preferring the glory of an open assault, the shining Silver Cavalry spring nimbly between Ryder Tower and the darkling woods to their south.

But the cavalry, in their brave eagerness, advance to within range of Sinistrum’s own horsemen, whose devastating impact completely wipes them out. Spotting the exposed flank of the High Elf spearmen, the horsemen prepare their next charge – but will the elite Storm Vermin be able to intercept them in time?

Without a moment to spare, the Savage Orcs, more at home among the trees than in the open, race through the dense woods to guard the horsemen's exposed flank.

In the meantime, the elven archers on the hill avenge some of their fallen brothers, smashing almost half the horsemen into splinters under a hail of unerring arrows.

The Storm Vermin now have a choice of targets: to flank the cavalry or smite the lightly armoured orcs head on? They decide that letting the orcs charge their own flanks would be too costly. However, the Great Horned Rat is an uncaring god, and a mere two expendable orcs are dispatched in exchange for the same number of precious rat warriors.

The Savage Orcs strike back at the remaining Storm Vermin, who, according to their warrior code, choose death before dishonour. The orcs shift to face the centre of the battlefield - can the expert marksmanship of the Coastal Warriors punish them for leaving the cover of the trees?

In the centre of the battlefield, the skeleton archers let fly with their biting arrows, felling a rank of stalwart elven spearmen.

The elite Black Orcs march towards the bridge, under the shadow of the approaching ice phoenix.

The skeleton horsemen continue their charge before the spearmen can turn to face them. Combined with another volley of arrows, it proves too much for the beleaguered elves.

But, in his eagerness, Sinistrum overextends and the Skaven Weapons Team is only too happy to punish him, wiping out every last horseman and leaving the foul liche on his own.

On the northern flank, the artillery softens up the orc spears, before the Doom Wheel crashes into their jagged wall of steel, but neither side emerges stronger.

The grim Black Orcs seize the initiative and charge Ryder’s prized ice phoenix. Their heavy axes butcher the beautiful creature in a cruel and bloody assault.

With the threat of the phoenix’s counter-charge removed, the bats are free to swoop across the river and into the Doom Wheel’s side, shaking its resolve with their unquenchable thirst for blood, then routing it in a second attack before its crew can steady their nerves.

Rat Ogres, orc spears and skeleton chariot manoeuvre into supporting positions.

The Rat Ogres catch the skeleton chariot, despite the shifting quicksand its magic conjures beneath their feet, and, seemingly surprised by their own strength, smash it into shards of bone and dust.

The boar riders rush to fill the gap, careering recklessly into the Rat Ogres. The towering vermin withstand the onslaught, strike back, and when the dust settles, the orcs are no more.

The surprising might of the Rat Ogres gives the bats pause, and they land by the riverside waypoint, rather than engage in a risky attack.

The skeletons and orcs have kept control of two of this side's three objectives, the Skaven and High Elves just one.

Meanwhile, back in the centre, the Black Orcs continue their rampage, turning on the rear of the weapons team and saving the liche from his second death. One ratman survives, beating a hasty retreat to the tower, which the Night Scouts have just vacated for the forest. However, Sinistrum gives chase, and so the tower ends under neither side’s control.

The Night Scouts come out of the shadows to charge the Savage Orcs, hoping to prevent them from controlling the forest's objective. Their flashing daggers are too fast for the eye to see, and they slay all but a handful.

A final volley from the elves wipes out the remaining unarmoured orcs, and so the forest, like the tower, ends under no one’s control.

Result

The battle is over. Despite heavy losses, Sinistrum and Jed have triumphed.
